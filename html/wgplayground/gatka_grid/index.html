HOW TO PLAY

🖱️
Drag-Select: Click and drag box to select your fighters (white ring outline).
🎯
Move / Target: Left-click on grid or enemy to issue command. Selection auto-clears!
⌨️
Camera: Use WASD or Arrow Keys to pan camera, Mouse Wheel to zoom.
⚡
Gatka Flow Meter: Reach 10 kills to fill the meter and unlock the popping ⚡ ACTIVATE GATKA FLOW button for 10s of +50% speed and 2x attack rate!
➕ / ⬆️
Map Nodes: Stand inside to capture. Heart (+) heals +10% HP/s. Spawn (▲) increases base spawn rate by 30%!
👥
Population Cap: Tailored max active fighters per faction.
