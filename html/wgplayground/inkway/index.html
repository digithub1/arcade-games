Game Objects Guide

💡 Pro Tip: In some levels, there may be trap props! To get 3 stars, find the clever path. Following trap routes will either make you lose stars or fail the level!

Drawn Ink Line

Draw custom ramps, bridges, and barriers for your vehicle. Every line consumes ink from your budget!

Car Start

Starting position where your vehicle spawns at the beginning of the level.

Ground

Solid terrain block used to build solid ground structures and obstacles.

Platform

Thin floating static platform for landing or driving across gaps.

Spike

Dangerous hazard spike! Touching spikes breaks your vehicle instantly.

Lava

Lava block hazard! Contact destroys your vehicle immediately.

Spring

Launches your vehicle high into the air when driven over.

Booster

Applies a strong directional speed boost to launch your car forward.

Gravity Flipper

Flips gravity upside down (or back to normal) when passing through the center zone.

Crumble

Fragile platform that breaks apart shortly after your vehicle lands on it.

Portal In

Entry portal. Driving into this blue portal teleports your car to the Portal Out location.

Portal Out

Exit portal. Your vehicle emerges from this orange portal while maintaining its speed and momentum.

Finish

Guide your car into the checkered goal zone to win and complete the level.
